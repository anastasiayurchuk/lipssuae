Beauty that cares.

LIPSS is a modern lip care brand where beauty meets care.

Our signature product is the Lipper — a lip gloss that has become a favourite among tens of thousands of girls around the world.

We created LIPSS because we wanted lip care to be something you actually look forward to using. Something with a great texture, a good scent, beautiful colours and a formula that takes care of your lips.

LIPSS — beauty that cares.

Meet the Lipper

More than a gloss.

The Lipper is what started it all.

It gives your lips the shine you want while caring for them at the same time. Every detail has been considered — from the texture and scent to the colour and the formula.

Because we don't think you should have to choose between looking good and taking care of your lips.

What's inside

Ceramides

Hydration

Ceramides help hydrate the lips and support their natural lipid barrier, leaving them soft and comfortable.

Peptides + vitamin E

Recovery

Peptides and vitamin E help care for delicate lip skin and support its elasticity.

Care + gloss

Care

Our formula brings together everyday lip care and a glossy finish, so your lips look good and feel good too.
